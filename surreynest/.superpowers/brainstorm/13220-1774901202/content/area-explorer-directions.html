What we are optimizing for

  • Easy for non-technical users to understand
  • Interactive enough to compare areas confidently
  • Natural handoff from area research to property decisions

What we want to avoid

  • Looking like a police dashboard
  • Overwhelming people with raw categories and jargon
  • Breaking away from SurreyNest's warm amber visual style
